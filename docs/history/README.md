# Historical records

These task reports, audits, design decisions and research notes describe implementation
stages. They are preserved for provenance, not as the current product contract. A statement
of support, an unfinished item or a measurement in one of these records may have been
superseded. Original filenames are retained.

For current behavior use:

- [Compatibility](../compatibility.md)
- [Current development state](../current-development-state.md)
- [Coverage model](../coverage-map-model.md) and [schema-v3 map format](../v3-map-format.md)
- [Gradle integration](../../smart-test-picker/README.md)
- [Maven integration](../../smart-test-picker-maven/README.md)
- [CLI fragment joining](../join-fragments-cli.md)

The `ei-*` records explain the incremental introduction of executable identities and
schema-v3 orchestration. The `mm-decision-*` record captures the earlier model decision.
The `task*` files retain their original stage-specific contracts and findings; the ASM
research summary is the historical narrative, not a replacement for the current collector
and finalization rules.
