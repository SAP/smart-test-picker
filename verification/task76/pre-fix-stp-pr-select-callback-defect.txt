Observed pre-fix logic:

  InventoryOutcome outcome = failure instanceof InventoryOperationException typed
      ? typed.outcome()
      : InventoryOutcome.INVENTORY_UNAVAILABLE;
  JenkinsPolicyAdapter.inventory(outcome, PolicyContext.PR_SELECTION, true)

BodyExecutionCallback.onFailure receives an arbitrary Throwable from the invoked Pipeline body.
Therefore a build-tool, user-body, plugin, or internal failure that is not an
InventoryOperationException was reclassified as INVENTORY_UNAVAILABLE and supplied an
unconditionally true full-suite safety hint. CentralFailurePolicy could consequently choose
RUN_FULL_SUITE for a failure that did not prove inventory-only optimization unavailability.

Required correction: preserve known InventoryOperationException outcomes, but normalize every
untyped callback failure to UNKNOWN with fullSuitePossible=false and let CentralFailurePolicy
choose FAIL_BUILD.
